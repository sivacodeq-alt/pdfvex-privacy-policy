How PDFVex handles PDF documents, photos, scanning, device settings and Google AdMob advertising.

At a glance. PDFVex does not require registration, use Firebase or offer cloud PDF backup in this release. Your documents are saved on your device. Google AdMob may independently process device and advertising-related information to show ads.

1. About PDFVex

PDFVex is an Android PDF utility for opening, scanning, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing and sharing documents. This policy describes our data practices and the third-party services used by the app.

2. PDF documents and local storage

When you import or create a PDF, PDFVex saves an app-managed copy in private storage on your device. The app may also save document names, sizes, recent activity and other document-library metadata locally. We do not intentionally upload your PDF contents or scanned images to a PDFVex-operated server for document processing.

Imported source files are not changed by importing them. You may explicitly share or export files to other applications, storage locations or cloud services that you choose. Those copies then follow the recipient's or destination's policies.

3. Selected files, images and scanner

PDFVex uses Android file/image selection to access only the documents and photos you choose for supported operations. When you use scanning, cropping or text-recognition features, the app may access the camera and use Android or Google Play services scanning/text-recognition components.

We do not intentionally transmit selected document content to our own servers. Underlying platform components can download software modules or handle technical information in accordance with their providers' policies. Permissions and access depend on the feature you select and your Android version.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

PDFVex uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banners, native ads and interstitials. AdMob may automatically collect and share some device and usage information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention, including:

  • IP address, which can reveal approximate location;
  • Android advertising ID and other device/app identifiers;
  • user interactions such as ad views and taps;
  • app and SDK performance or diagnostic information.

Google determines how this information is processed according to its policies, applicable law and device or consent settings. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google My Ad Center. PDFVex does not sell your PDF document contents to advertisers.

5. App preferences and account information

PDFVex may keep onboarding completion, theme preferences, PDF library entries and related settings in local app storage. This release has no Google Login, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Firebase Analytics or developer-operated user account database. You do not need to provide us with an email address or password to use PDFVex.

6. Information you choose to share

If you tap Share or export a file, Android may deliver that file to the external app or destination you select. Review the destination's policies before sharing sensitive documents. We do not provide your PDF file contents to AdMob for advertising. Google may process technical advertising information as described above.

7. Retention and deletion

App-managed documents and local settings generally remain on the device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Removing a file from My Documents deletes the PDFVex-managed copy, not the original imported file or separately exported copy. Clearing storage or uninstalling the app generally removes its private on-device files, but copies may remain in Android backups, third-party locations or exports.

Advertising-related information held by Google follows Google's separate retention and deletion policies. For detailed instructions, visit the PDFVex Data Deletion page.

8. Security

PDFVex saves managed PDFs within private Android application storage and checks managed file paths before deleting files. Protecting your phone with a screen lock is recommended. No system can guarantee absolute security. Google handles its SDK network communication according to its own security practices.

9. Children's privacy

PDFVex is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically directed at children. We do not provide accounts for children. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns can use the contact details below.

10. Your privacy choices

You can choose which documents to import, delete app-managed documents, clear the app's storage or uninstall PDFVex. Manage advertising identifiers and personalization through Android Settings and Google's ad controls. Privacy rights and applicable options differ by location.

Because PDFVex does not keep users' documents on a developer server, we cannot remotely access or delete files that exist only on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PDFVex's features, third-party SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated on this page when changes are made.
